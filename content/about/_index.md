I currently work as a Senior AI/ML Engineer, building practical data science and machine learning solutions across domains such as manufacturing, energy, finance, logistics, and pharma. I have worked with both small and large teams to deliver AI/ML and analytics projects using modern tools and technologies.

My background spans NLP, machine learning, and optimisation-driven numerical simulations. I enjoy being involved across the full lifecycle of a project—from problem framing and data understanding to stakeholder communication and solution deployment. I am especially drawn to the intersection of technical depth and real-world impact.

These days, I mostly work in Python, though I am also comfortable with R. I am currently exploring Rust and enjoy learning languages that help me build more robust and efficient solutions.

Outside of work, I am also a playwright and deeply love theatre. I am always happy to connect with people in Pune for recommendations or a good conversation.
